THE OBJECTIVE

Play for 21.

FOUR STEPS. ONE GOAL.

01 / 04

The deal

The dealer has a hidden card, while your first two cards are visible. Your goal is to get closer to 21 than the dealer without going over.

02 / 04

Make your move

Select Hit to draw another card or Stay to finish your hand and reveal the dealer's hidden card.

03 / 04

Know the cards

Cards 2–10 count at face value. J, Q and K each count as 10. An Ace counts as 11 or 1 as needed.

04 / 04

The outcome

After you stay, totals are compared. The higher non-busting total wins; equal totals tie. A total above 21 is a bust.
