PEOPLE’S DAILY / NEW MEDIA CENTERBEIJING, CHINA

People’s Daily.

Video Intern · English Client / Video Team

Producing clear, accessible English-language stories from Chinese news footage for audiences beyond the original domestic context.

WHAT I WORKED ON

My primary responsibilities were video editing and English subtitle production: repurposing source footage, refining pacing, reorganising on-screen text and adapting visual content for an international readership. I contributed to short news videos, a documentary podcast and special-format productions as part of the English-client team.

THE EDITORIAL PRACTICE

This work made cross-cultural communication tangible. Editing was not simply translation: it meant understanding the context of a story, choosing what viewers need to see, and ensuring captions and visual rhythm work together. The newsroom experience also provided fieldwork context for my undergraduate research.

XINHUA NEWS AGENCY / XINHUA FINANCEFINANCE & INFORMATION

Xinhua Finance.

Intern · Financial News Writing & Public-Opinion Monitoring

Working at the intersection of financial reporting and media analysis: writing news for the public while monitoring how information circulates.

FINANCIAL NEWS WRITING

I independently researched and wrote five bylined financial-news articles, covering policy finance, banking results, bond markets and anti-fraud cases. The writing process combined public-source research, numerical verification, policy context and editorial review.

PUBLIC-OPINION MONITORING

Alongside reporting, I drafted approximately 105 daily, weekly and monthly monitoring reports for a confidential institutional client, following editorial review. The process involved collecting publicly available discussions, classifying material, comparing patterns and developing concise risk assessments. Client identities and report contents are not published here.

CHINA MEDIA GROUP / CCTV FINANCE CHANNELCCTV-2

CCTV Finance.

Intern · Editorial Research & Broadcast Scriptwriting

Turning complex developments in advanced manufacturing into clear, human-centred stories for a general television audience.

EDITORIAL RESEARCH

I prepared five structured profiles on specialised manufacturing innovators, organising public-source information about technical achievements, industry context and innovation. The coverage spanned power equipment, precision cutting tools, heavy forging, frequency conversion and optical materials.

BROADCAST-STYLE WRITING

I also developed five television-script drafts for the “Specialized & Innovative · Manufacturing Power” editorial series, learning to bring technical details into a broadcast-friendly narrative. The work centred on desk research, fact-checking and scriptwriting, rather than field interviews or video editing.
